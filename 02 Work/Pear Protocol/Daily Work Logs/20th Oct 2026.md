## Agent honesty: stop the model inventing refusals and "nothing found"

- [#1147](https://github.com/pear-protocol/agent-pear-v2/pull/1147) (07:08 UTC): web users with no wallet linked asked to open a single-asset trade and got invented refusals such as "Pear only supports two-leg pairs". A composer-only fix was tried and dropped: it still repeated the refusal in 6 of 6 samples.
  - Fix: a trade command without `tradeExecution` goes to generalChat alone; the pair node answers `single_asset_query` for one-asset queries.
