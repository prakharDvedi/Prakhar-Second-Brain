## v2s vs old, what has improved so far (from the 5-run read, 85 calls per arm, 17 assets)

- Bullish while falling vs benchmark over 7d (49 cases): `old` 33 (67%), `v2s` 5 (10%).
- Direction calls agreeing with the 7d move: `old` 48 of 84 (57%), `v2s` 28 of 33 (85%).
- Assets that flipped direction on identical inputs: `old` 7 of 17, `new` 2, `v2s` 0.
- Neutral or mixed calls (of 85): `old` 1, `v2s` 52 (61%).
- Not shown: accuracy (+1d on the 10-05 runs, `v2s` 50% on 14 scored vs `old` 42%, too few calls), and nothing beats momentum. `v2s` gives a direction on only 39% of calls, so baskets get less lean to use; how baskets and chat treat neutral is unchecked.
- The gain is the V2 prompt bundle (four edits at once), not one change. The data layer alone (`new`) was worse than `old`.
- Leaning: build on `v2s`, drop `new`. A leaning, not a decision. Next: +3d/+7d scores (8-13 Oct), then A1a (HL funding/OI) and A1b (Coinglass) stacked on `v2s`.
