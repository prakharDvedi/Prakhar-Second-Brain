## Funding dead band backtest (`eth-funding-flip`)

Window 18 Aug to 24 Oct (Elfa's fire window), ETH settled hourly funding from Hyperliquid `fundingHistory` (1608 hours, 107 negative). "Real" = average of the fire hour and next 3 settled hours < 0. Regime = 4+ consecutive negative hours (8 found). Simulated hourly with the 4h book cooldown (the poller reads the live rate every minute, which wobbles more, so small bands look better here than they will live).

| rule | fires | real | regimes caught | median lag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cross below 0 (today) | 40 | 15 (38%) | 7/8 | 0h |
| cross below −2% APR | 31 | 17 (55%) | 8/8 | 1h |
| cross below −3% APR | 28 | 16 (57%) | 8/8 | 1h |
| cross below −5% APR | 21 | 13 (62%) | 7/8 | 1h |
| cross below −8% APR | 11 | 8 (73%) | 7/8 | 2h |
| hysteresis ±3 (re-arm above +3) | 25 | 14 (56%) | 7/8 | 1h |

- Hysteresis is no better than a shifted threshold, so the simple shift wins.
- Elfa: 94 fires, only 18 sustained, settled funding at the fire hour had median **+5.4% APR** (p10 −2.0, p90 +11.0), so Elfa mostly fired while funding was clearly positive.
- Recommendation: move the book value from 0 to −3% APR (config only). Small sample (8 regimes), re-check in dev.
- Data in scratchpad `eth-funding.json`, `elfa-funding-fires.json`, script `fund-band.js`.

### Decision: a first reading never fires, personal rules included

- Our poller fires only on a false to true edge. A new alert's first check records state and stays silent, even if the condition is already true. Elfa fired at arm time in that case.
- Decided to keep ours. A person sets a watch when they see the setup, so it being already true at that moment is a rare coincidence. The user reads a watch as "from now on, tell me when it happens". It also stops a lost Redis state from replaying every true rule at once.
- Cost of the decision: some of the 24 "Elfa only, true on Hyperliquid" replay fires were already-true-at-start fires we will not send. The exact share is not measured.
- Recorded as a code comment in `math-shadow.processor.ts` (uncommitted, with the cap).

### Decision: `maxTriggers` caps personal alerts only

- Personal: at most `maxTriggers` (3) fires per 168h window, counted from the first fire. Matches the rule's weekly rebuild and the stated intent in `buildStatementSignalEntry`.
- Curated: no cap, cooldown only. Their 720h lifetime as a window would cap crude-oil-shock at 3 per 30 days, Elfa fired it 58 times in about 10 weeks.
- Interest and custom alerts are news only, not on the math poller, so out of scope.
- In `math-shadow.processor.ts` with a spec (uncommitted). Signals plus poller specs 723/723.
