### Update: which assets each arm picked (3 paired A1 runs)

A pick is a direction called in 2 or more of the 3 runs. Rel 7d is the asset's 7d move minus its benchmark's, in pp. BTC is the benchmark, so it's left out of the trend counts.

| arm | long | short | with 7d trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| `old` | NEAR, BTC, ZEC, PLTR, AMZN, TSLA, GOOGL, META, NVDA | LIT, ENA, HYPE, ETH, XRP, SOL, PUMP, MSTR | 10 of 16 |
| `v2s` | NEAR, PLTR, AMZN | LIT, BTC, ETH, SOL | 5 of 6 |
| `v2s+A1a` | NEAR, PLTR, AMZN | ETH, SOL | 5 of 5 |
| `v2s+A1ab` | NEAR, AMZN | BTC, ETH, SOL | 4 of 4 |

- **`old`** calls all 17, partly by type: long on 6 of 7 stocks, short on most crypto. It changes direction between runs (ZEC S/B/B, META B/B/S).
- **The `v2s` family** only commits on large caps with clear moves: long on the top winners (NEAR +19.3, PLTR +10.5, AMZN +4.0) and short on weak large caps (ETH -3.8, SOL -4.8). Neutral on anything under about 3pp, including most stocks, HYPE and ZEC. That's close to momentum on the extremes.
- **Differences from `v2s`:**
  - LIT: `v2s` was short twice while LIT was +4.8pp; both A1 arms dropped it. It was `v2s`'s only call against the trend.
  - NEAR: bullish 3 of 3 under A1a and A1ab, 2 of 3 under `v2s`. NEAR has OI +13% in 1d and +22% in 7d.
  - BTC: short twice under `v2s`, neutral under A1a, short twice under A1ab. A1ab sees 7d long liquidations of $447M vs $137M short, and OI down 5.6% over 7d.
  - PLTR dropped to neutral twice under A1ab. It's a stock, so A1ab sees the same data as A1a there; that's noise.
- **PUMP** (-9.1pp, the worst performer): no `v2s`-family arm shorted it; each was bullish in one run. Its top-trader share is getting longer (73% vs a 65% average). If PUMP keeps falling, that's a point against reading top-trader positioning.
- **All four arms agree** on ETH and SOL short and AMZN long.
- **Caveats:** one day and a few assets. Agreeing with the trend says the calls are consistent, not that they're right. Accuracy comes from the 13, 15 and 19 Oct scoring.
