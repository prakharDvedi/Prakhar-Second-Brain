The email structure is:

1. Greet the hiring contact by first name and say “Hope you're doing well.”
2. Introduce Prakhar as a fourth-year B.Tech student at IIIT Bhopal.
3. For a specific role, mention the listing and one requirement that matches the selected profiles. If the role is not verified on an official source, say “I came across a listing for…” rather than claiming the company is hiring now.

6. Include the “Proficient in” table with two resume-based, four-word highlights per candidate and the candidate's public Drive link.
7. Include the placement brochure as a hyperlink when relevant. The previous email linked the brochure; it did not attach a file.
8. Close directly and politely. Keep the email crisp and semi-professional.

Natural variation in wording is fine when it reflects the recipient and role. Keep all claims accurate. Do not deliberately add typos, random capitalization, awkward phrasing, or altered meaning to evade spam or bot detection.
